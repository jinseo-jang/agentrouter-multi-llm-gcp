08  /  REQUIREMENTS

A wrong pick costs more: four things a router needs

If an easy-looking request goes to a small model and fails, retry costs can wipe out the savings

Google Cloud  |  AgentRouter Research  |  Research date 2026-10-01

09

01

Decide per session

Not Diamond picks models on the quality and cost of the whole session, not just the next request. Cognition switches models mid-session without losing the cache.

Source: Not Diamond  ·  Cognition

02

Learn from real usage

Cursor evaluated its router with online A/B tests over millions of requests instead of offline evals, using user satisfaction as the reward.

Source: Cursor

03

Include open-weight models in the pool

In Not Diamond's test, adding open-weight candidates raised performance by 3.6% and grew savings from 39% to 66%.

Source: Not Diamond

04

Data boundaries and governance

Digital Realty runs internal data it will not send to frontier models on its own private open-weight models. Routing rules need data classification.

Source: FT summary

A router that mixed in open-weight models was cheaper and more accurate than one limited to Anthropic models.

Capture: Not Diamond Blog, SWE-PolyBench-Verified
